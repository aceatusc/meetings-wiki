# Productivity-Creativity Tradeoff

## Definition

The productivity-creativity tradeoff is the tension between LLMs helping users produce faster, more complete, or more correct outputs while reducing human ideation, aha moments, or creative ownership.

## In The Programming Study

The meeting suggests using this as the core result from RQ1 and motivation for RQ2. LLM-assisted participants produced more correct and complete responses under time constraints, but solution diversity did not clearly increase. Process measures suggested less ideation time and fewer creative moments.

## Presentation Use

The tradeoff should appear before the process analysis. It gives the audience a reason to ask why the pattern occurs, which leads naturally into ideation time, creative moments, and collaboration modes.
