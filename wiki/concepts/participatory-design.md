# Participatory Design

## Definition

Participatory design or co-design is used here as a lightweight way to avoid design fixation, validate prototype ideas, and gather expert redesign suggestions.

## Current Use

The team discussed expert review with a prototype, video, screenshot, or sketchable artifact. A short follow-up discussion can ask what works, what does not, and how participants would redesign the tool.

By 2026-03-16, the transcript says 8 participatory design/formative sessions had been conducted for the prototype.

## Study Guidance

- Keep the procedure within IRB/formative-study boundaries.
- Do not oversell the co-design process as a heavy empirical method if it is lightweight.
- Use sketches or screenshots when useful for the paper.
- Extract design goals and validate whether core features are accepted.
