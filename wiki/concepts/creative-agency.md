# Creative Agency

## Definition

Creative agency is the user's sense of ownership, control, and active participation in creative problem solving.

## In The Programming Study

The study's agency concern is that LLMs may remove users from the creative work itself. Participants may bypass ideation, accept generated paths of least resistance, and experience fewer aha moments even when outputs are more complete.

## Design Implication

The target is not to reject LLM productivity gains. The design goal is to preserve productivity while supporting genuine human ideation, ownership, and moments of discovery.
